WEB-412

Safari: date picker opens behind the booking modal

Status
Priority
Assignee
Labels
BugCustomer
Cycle

Description

On Safari 17 and 18, opening the date picker inside the booking modal renders the calendar underneath the modal's backdrop, so guests can't pick a date.

  1. Open any booking page in Safari.
  2. Choose Pick another day.
  3. The calendar appears dimmed behind the overlay and ignores clicks.

Likely the picker's portal mounts at z-index: 50 while the modal sits at 400.
