Lemon ricotta pancakes

Soft, golden pancakes with creamy ricotta and a bright little lift of lemon.

25 minutes
Serves 4
Jump to recipe

Check off ingredients as you go.

Ingredients

Serves 4

Method

  1. Whisk the wet ingredients

    In a bowl, whisk together the ricotta, milk, eggs, and lemon zest.

  2. Fold in the dry ingredients

    Add the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Gently fold into the ricotta mixture until just combined.

  3. Give it a moment

    Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.

  4. Cook until golden

    Butter a pan over medium heat. Add the batter in portions and cook until bubbles form on the surface. Flip once and cook the other side until golden.
