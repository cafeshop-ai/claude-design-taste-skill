LET’S MAKE SOMETHING GOOD

A stack of sunshine

4 simple steps
  1. 1

    Start with the good stuff

    In a large bowl, whisk together the ricotta, milk, eggs, and lemon zest until well combined.

  2. 2

    Keep it light

    Add the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until just combined. A few lumps are a good thing.

  3. 3

    A little moment of rest

    Let the batter rest for 5 minutes. Put the kettle on, find your favorite plates, and let the batter do its thing.

  4. 4

    Golden, then flip

    Warm a little butter in a pan over medium heat. Spoon in the batter and cook until bubbles form on the surface. Flip once and cook until golden and cooked through.

A little table wisdom

Go easy on the mixing. A gentle fold keeps these pancakes beautifully fluffy. Serve warm, with whatever makes your morning sweeter.
