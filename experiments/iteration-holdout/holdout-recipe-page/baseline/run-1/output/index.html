Let’s get cooking

25 minutes, start to finish

  1. Start with the good stuff

    In a large bowl, whisk together the ricotta, milk, eggs, and lemon zest until well combined. A few little ricotta curds are perfectly lovely.

  2. Keep it light

    Add the flour, sugar, baking powder, and a pinch of salt. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until just combined. Don’t overmix.

  3. A little pause

    Let the batter rest for 5 minutes. Just enough time to put the kettle on and warm up your pan.

  4. Golden, then flip

    Heat a little butter in a pan over medium heat. Spoon in the batter and cook until bubbles form on the surface. Flip once, then cook the other side until golden and cooked through.

A note from the kitchen

Gentle folding is the secret to fluffy pancakes. Serve them warm, straight from the pan. A drizzle of maple syrup and a little extra lemon zest make a lovely finishing touch.
