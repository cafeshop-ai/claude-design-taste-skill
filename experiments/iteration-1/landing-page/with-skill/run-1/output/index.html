Is Tidepool right for a small gym?

That’s who it’s designed for. Tidepool focuses on the everyday essentials for independent climbing gyms: managing memberships, organizing classes, and welcoming day-pass visitors.

Can I manage memberships and day passes together?

Yes. Memberships and day passes live in the same workspace, so your front desk can look after regulars and first-time visitors without switching between tools.

What about classes with limited spaces?

Keep an eye on class capacity and bookings in one place. Try the class-booking preview above to see how available spaces change as someone books a session.

Can I try it before creating an account?

Absolutely. Open the sample workspace and explore all three workflows. It uses example data, needs no account or payment information, and resets when you reload the page.
